# AMZ Store pre-launch checklist

## Business and legal
- [ ] Confirm registered business identity and contact details for the website.
- [ ] Publish accurate delivery, returns, cancellation, warranty, privacy and cookie information.
- [ ] Confirm consumer-law wording for used goods and disclose known faults clearly.
- [ ] Confirm VAT registration/treatment and display prices consistently.
- [ ] Obtain permission/rights for every product image, logo and supplier feed.

## Products and suppliers
- [ ] Verify part number and vehicle compatibility before listing.
- [ ] Photograph the actual used item and every material defect.
- [ ] Separate AMZ-owned stock from supplier stock.
- [ ] Confirm supplier availability and dispatch SLA.
- [ ] Define who handles returns, warranty claims and refunds for partner stock.
- [ ] Keep acquisition costs and supplier data private.

## Commerce and security
- [ ] Configure live payment provider only after test transactions pass.
- [ ] Configure UK shipping zones, rates, parcel limits and dispatch cut-offs.
- [ ] Configure tax/VAT with professional advice.
- [ ] Configure transactional email and SPF/DKIM/DMARC for the sending domain.
- [ ] Enable SSL, automatic updates where appropriate, backups and security monitoring.
- [ ] Use strong administrator passwords and least-privilege accounts.
- [ ] Test basket, checkout, stock reservation, cancellation, refund and order emails.

## Integrations
- [ ] Choose a licensed UK registration/vehicle-data provider before enabling number-plate lookup.
- [ ] Decide whether supplier stock will remain manually confirmed or be synchronised through an authorised feed/API.
- [ ] Decide how competitor price references will be researched, sourced, dated and reviewed.
- [ ] Never scrape websites in breach of their terms or display stale prices as live offers.

## Quality assurance
- [ ] Test on mobile, tablet and desktop.
- [ ] Test keyboard navigation, contrast, forms and error states.
- [ ] Test product search and no-results messaging.
- [ ] Check all links, contact forms and legal pages.
- [ ] Run a full staging order before DNS changes.
