# AMZ Store product-entry workflow

## Inventory model

AMZ Store supports two stock sources:

1. **AMZ Store owned stock** — a physical item held by the business. Enter its real quantity/location and use actual photographs of the item being sold.
2. **External UK supplier stock** — an item offered by a breaker or supplier. Record the supplier and supplier reference. Do not represent it as AMZ-owned stock. Confirm live availability before accepting an order and before dispatch.

## Used-part condition grades

- **Grade A — excellent used:** minimal cosmetic wear. State what has been inspected/tested.
- **Grade B — good used:** normal wear; disclose all material marks and missing clips/fixings.
- **Grade C — fair used:** noticeable wear or defects; photograph and describe them prominently.

These grades are AMZ Store's own descriptions, not a statutory industry certification. Define and apply them consistently. Do not claim an item is tested unless an appropriate test was actually performed.

## Recommended product title

`[Make/model/generation] [part name] [left/right/front/rear] [OE reference]`

Example format only: `Ford Focus Mk3 Left Headlamp — OE reference 123456`.

## Essential information

- Part name and exact part/OE number.
- Vehicle make, model, generation, year range, engine/trim code and relevant variant.
- Left/right/front/rear position where applicable.
- New or used status and used condition grade.
- Actual photographs, including close-ups of defects, labels and connectors.
- Inspection/test status and the test actually performed.
- Known defects, missing pieces and included accessories.
- Stock source (owned or partner supplier), quantity and location.
- Supplier name/reference for partner stock.
- Selling price, VAT treatment, delivery charge and dispatch estimate.
- Actual warranty terms and returns information.

## Price comparison policy

The AMZ fields store administrator-entered reference values. Before displaying a comparison, match the same part number or exact specification, vehicle compatibility, side, condition, included accessories and delivery/VAT basis. Record the source and date checked in the product description or internal notes. Distinguish completed-sale data from asking prices. Do not label a manual reference as a live market average or guaranteed cheapest price.

## Margin calculation

Calculate expected contribution using the real acquisition cost plus inspection/preparation, packaging, delivery subsidy, payment fees and any other direct costs. Account for VAT and overheads separately with your accountant. The plugin stores supplier/acquisition cost for internal admin use; do not expose cost data to customers.
